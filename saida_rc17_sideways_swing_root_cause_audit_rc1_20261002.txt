HL_RULE = >=2 confirmed swing lows and latest > previous; else false.
LH_RULE = >=2 confirmed swing highs and latest < previous; else false.
LL_RULE = >=2 confirmed swing lows and latest < previous; else false.
UP_RULE = higher_high and higher_low (HH and HL).
DOWN_RULE = lower_high and lower_low (LH and LL), after UP branch.
SIDEWAYS_RULE = at least one swing high OR one swing low, and neither UP nor DOWN pair. Both swing types are NOT required. No minimum two-swings requirement for validity.
UNKNOWN_RULE = neither swing list has any element -> trend UNKNOWN, confidence 0, valid false, early return. Early market/history guards return context without computing; in AnalysisPipeline context.clear_results previously sets UNKNOWN/valid=false.
VALID_RULE = after guards, result.clear and no-swings early return, RC17 ends with result.valid=True. Thus all nonempty-swing cases reaching end, including SIDEWAYS and only one swing type, are valid. No requirement for BOS, CHOCH or directional trend.
LAST_HIGH_RULE = after clear, iff swing_highs nonempty: swing_high=last_high=swing_highs[-1]. Otherwise swing_high remains None and last_high=0.0.
LAST_LOW_RULE = after clear, iff swing_lows nonempty: swing_low=last_low=swing_lows[-1]. Otherwise swing_low remains None and last_low=0.0.
LAST_HIGH_ZERO_MEANING = reset/default sentinel when not overwritten; snapshot float(structure.last_high or 0.0) collapses missing/falsy to zero. Zero does not certify absence by itself because Candle/RC17 do not enforce positive swing prices.
LAST_LOW_ZERO_MEANING = reset/default when no low assigned; snapshot float(structure.last_low or 0.0) also collapses None/falsy.
